Orders — repair orders by their order date. Warranty / Service — orders with a warranty claim / a service (BSI) claim, from the order or from the Incadea claim upload. Warranty only / Service only / Both — which claims the order has (an order needs one or the other, or both). Pending — no claim of either kind yet and not returned to the advisor. Answered — BMW's credit note received. Sent back — BMW rejected at least one line (SAP status Error); BMW return rate = sent back ÷ answered. Cut — BMW paid only part (status Warning). Hidden by default — ⚙ Columns. Returned to advisor — not a warranty case (internal, customer pays…): no claim, a return date set; return rate = returned ÷ orders. Picked up / Waiting pick-up — received by the warranty admin on the 📥 Pick-up page, or still to receive (not returned); days to pick-up = average days from the order's date to its pick-up. Days to claim — average days from the order date to its first claim.

📞 Mitel / softphone click-to-dial

        
Works with any softphone registered as your computer's default tel: handler — Mitel One, MiCollab, MiVoice Office, MiCloud Connect, etc. Each user sets their Mitel app as default phone handler on their device (one-time OS setting).

      

      

        
        
        
Used for numbers typed without a country prefix. Lebanese number 03 555 101 dials as +96103555101. Use 33 for France, 971 for UAE, etc.

      

      

        
        
      

      

        
        
        
Use {number} placeholder (with + prefix) or {number_no_plus} (digits only).
Examples: callto:{number} · sip:{number_no_plus}@pbx.bashen.com · mitelone://call/{number}
